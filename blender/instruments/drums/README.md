# Drum kit

Bill Kreutzmann's kit in summer 1974: butcher-block concert toms. It's
modelled as it stood before the show at Dillon Stadium, Hartford, on 31 July
1974. The same kit, as far as a partial view shows, is at Winterland on
16 October 1974.

**The kit changed during the summer.** It was red on 25 May and, probably, on 16 June. It was butcher block by 31 July. So the model is right for 31 July to 20 October, and wrong for the first weeks of the window. See `docs/qa-findings.md` §7.

**Also consulted, not seen directly:**
- A drum-forum post, seen only as a search summary because the forum blocks automated readers, says he played a wine-red Ludwig early in 1974. It says he moved in summer 1974 to "the butcher block concert tom kit… the one seen in the Grateful Dead movie". The photographs agree.
- A mid-1970s Zildjian guide, as quoted by Reverb, lists his cymbals as Avedis Zildjians: 14" Rock hi-hats and an 18" crash.

## What's in the kit (the pre-show photograph)

- **Bass drum**, 24" (see *Scale*), with a white coated Remo front head and no port.
- **Four concert toms**, single-headed with open bottoms:
  - two on the bass drum's rail, about 13" and 14"
  - two on stands to the audience's left, about 12" and 10", stepping down
- **Cymbals:**
  - hi-hats at 34", on the audience's right
  - a crash high centre-left
  - a flat cymbal high on the far left
  - a dark cymbal high right
- **Throne:** a round patterned seat, behind the kit and a little to the audience's left.

**Scale.** A straight-on photograph can only give sizes relative to one
another, so one size must be assumed. The bass drum is taken as 24". That also
fits the 14" hi-hats the Zildjian guide lists: they measure about 13" at the
bass drum's scale, and they stand farther back. Every other size follows from
it; tom sizes are rounded to standard concert-tom sizes.

## Outstanding

1. **The make and the badges.** They need a closer photograph.
2. **Front-to-back layout.** It needs a second, side-on view, such as a Winterland photograph or a frame from *The Grateful Dead Movie*.
3. **The snare**, hidden in every photograph.
4. **The Remo logo** on the front head isn't drawn.
5. **The dark cymbal's type**: the Grateful Dead Archive has a Zildjian "China Boy" from Kreutzmann's or Hart's kit, but it isn't tied to this date.
6. **Mics and the riser**: the drum mics and the kit's riser belong to the stage scene.
